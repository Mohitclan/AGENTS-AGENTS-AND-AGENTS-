# Songs for the office speaker

Put your own songs here: `.mp3`, `.m4a`, `.aac`, `.ogg`, `.opus`, `.wav` or `.flac` files. Then click the speaker on
the rug in the 3D office (or the ♪ Music button) and pick one.

The file name becomes the title: `01 - Badtameez Dil.mp3` shows as "Badtameez Dil". The folder is git-ignored, so
songs stay on this computer and are never committed or pushed.
