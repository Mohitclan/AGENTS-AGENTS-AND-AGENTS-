Rules:

- A deck is a source, not confirmed knowledge. Cite it as `knowledge/decks/<slug>.pdf p.N`. A fact from a deck reaches a customer only once its fact sheet is confirmed.
- Slides marked "illustrative" or "sample" show made-up data. Never quote their names, prices or numbers as ours.
- A deck may be attached to an email only when the person approving the send agrees to it, and the email names it.
- To replace a deck, put the new PDF here under the same name, regenerate its `.txt`, and re-check the fact sheet against it.
