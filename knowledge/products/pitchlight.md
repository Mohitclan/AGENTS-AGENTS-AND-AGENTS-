## One-liner
A private AI copilot for client calls: talking points, answers and client context appear on your screen during the call, only you can see them, and the recap and follow-up are written afterwards. [https://pitchlight.panscience.ai; knowledge/decks/pitchlight.pdf p.1, p.3, p.9]

## Problem it solves
When a client asks something hard, the person on the call is listening, talking, watching the clock and trying to remember the price, the proof and the next step all at once, and their mind goes blank. [deck p.2]

Supporting figures, as the deck gives them: 36% fear forgetting their words when presenting, 33% fear freezing in front of other people, 28% fear losing their train of thought. [deck p.2, citing "Preply survey of 2,007 people in the UK"; the survey itself not checked, so don't quote these until someone reads it]

## Limitations and known issues
- Chrome only. [told by escandita@panscience.ai on 2026-11-05; site "Works in Chrome"]
- Calls are transcribed, not recorded. [told by escandita@panscience.ai on 2026-11-05] Still not stated: where transcripts are processed and stored, which AI provider is used, and whether the other people on the call are told. Recording and transcription consent rules differ by country, so get this answered before outreach outside a person's own use.
- The practice app's scored review includes "video replay" (site), which suggests the practice app records the user's own video. That is the user rehearsing alone, not a client call.
