## One-liner
An A4 label designer: design a sticker sheet once, fill it from a spreadsheet, PDF or photo, and download an exact-size A4 PDF ready to print. [https://peelpress.panscience.ai; knowledge/decks/peelpress.pdf p.1]

## Problem it solves
Making shipping stickers, product tags and address labels means retyping names and addresses from a spreadsheet one label at a time, prints that drift off the sticker edges because the size isn't exact, and partly used sheets thrown away because the used labels can't be skipped. [knowledge/decks/peelpress.pdf p.2]

## Ideal customer profile
- **Region:** India first, since pricing is in INR. [told by escandita@panscience.ai on 2026-11-05; https://peelpress.panscience.ai pricing]

- **Buying triggers:** unknown. Hypothesis: printing labels every week, or a sale season with many orders.
- **Disqualifiers:** unknown. Hypothesis: anyone needing non-A4 label rolls (thermal printers), since the product is A4 only.

## Limitations and known issues
- A4 sheets only. [https://peelpress.panscience.ai; deck p.1]
- Free plan: up to 50 labels per download, 2 saved designs, 3 AI imports a month, and a small "Made with Peelpress" line in the margin. [site pricing]
- AI imports need a free sign-in. [site pricing]
- Where the AI step processes uploaded files, and which AI provider it uses, is not stated. Ask before selling to anyone careful about customer data.
