## Brand voice
- **Tone:** plain-spoken and confident. Short sentences, concrete outcomes, no hype.
- **Words we use:** plain words for what the product does and the result the customer gets: "saves", "set up in", "works with".
- **Words we avoid:** revolutionary, game-changing, cutting-edge, best-in-class, world-class, seamless, synergy, leverage (as a verb), "AI-powered" as a selling point on its own, guaranteed, and any claim that has no source.
- **Spelling:** Indian English, e.g. "organisation", "colour", "lakh" and "crore" for Indian amounts, ₹ for prices.
