## Questions for you

1. **Who runs the live demo?** One trial can't hold both a dry run and a live run (4 + 4 credits is more than 6). The options are in demo plan section 4:
   - (a) the prospect connects their repo on their own trial during the call;
   - (b) play a staff dry run as "made earlier" (the suggested default);
   - (c) a second staff trial account;
   - (d) the homepage sample videos.
2. **Who does the dry run?** It needs to check that a Go repo gives output worth showing and that pasting a third-party public repo URL works live. The code says it should.
3. **Is MotionRepo's invoice a GST tax invoice?** Until a source confirms it, the team says "₹3,199/month + 18% GST, paid by invoice".
4. **Should the tone names go into the fact sheet?** Polished, cinematic and the others come from code only. Until then, the presenter reads them off the screen.
5. **Is Deplexo contacted at all?** It is tier C at low confidence, with only general inboxes.

## Lessons recorded

- **demo-handler:** a claim that traces to a fact-sheet line still needs that line's label checked, because hypothesis lines can't be spoken to a prospect.
- **sales:** never claim your file matches another agent's file unless you read it.
- **evaluator** (its own memory): checks for demo packs, and recounting word limits before suggesting added words.
- **`lint-copy mine`:**
  - `unsourced-number` hit 15 times in 6 files. Most were mid-write saves; that is a known issue already on the board.
  - `placeholder` hit 3 times, all from the required `{{SENDER_*}}` fields.
