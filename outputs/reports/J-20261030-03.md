**The signal classes helped more than the gate did.** Every evidence item had a class, so the evaluator could go straight to the `observed_gap` items and test them. That is how the Groundrule error surfaced.

**What the gate can't see.** The test showed a Go from the gate means less than it appears to:

- It trusts the submitted score and doesn't read claims. Groundrule's Go rested on a false gap.
- It can't see size, founder status or a parent company, and it treats all email addresses alike.
- It doesn't check signal age, only `researched_at`.
- From reading the code: it doesn't flag evidence with no `signal` at all. No item here lacked one, so this didn't bite.
- With `--status all` it shows Go on a lead still `new`, as it does for Repowise today.
- It has no way to hold a lead. `coordinator.md` step 7 also offers only qualified or disqualified, and neither fitted Repowise.

Re-import also has limits. It can't change a lead's status or `disqualify_reason`, so I made those corrections with `set-status`. It merges evidence, so superseded claims stay in `pipeline/leads.jsonl`, including Groundrule's false gap and Repowise's wrong "Not found" item. Corrections sit beside them and the notes file lists them, but anyone reading `show` from the top meets the wrong claim first.
