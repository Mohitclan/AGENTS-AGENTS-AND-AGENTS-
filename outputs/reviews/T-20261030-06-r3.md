## Decision: Repowise, option (a)

Keep L-4d574242 `new` until a person has opened `youtube.com/@repowise`. No drafts in this run.

### The case for (b)

Option (b) is not unsafe, and it breaks no hard rule.

- A draft leaves nothing outside this folder.
- A person approves every send.
- The status can be undone: `qualified` → `disqualified` and `drafted` → `disqualified` are both allowed.

It would also give this test run a draft to look at. Those points are real, but they aren't enough.

### Does (b) weaken `qualified`? Yes.

- **What the word means.** The schema says `qualified` means "passed review". Round 2 said Repowise passes only after the channel check, so marking it now would mean "passed review, except for the thing review said it must pass".
- **Why Deplexo differs.** Deplexo is qualified with unknowns too, but they are of another kind. Its founder and size are gaps about how good the lead is, and a person can accept them. Repowise's open question tests the premise of the lead: is there a gap at all? Groundrule was disqualified on exactly this question.
- **The condition would not be enforced.** `gateOf` in `scripts/leads.mjs` reads suppression, status, contact, research age, tier and signal class. It doesn't read `ops/approvals.md`, and neither does `set-status`.
  - Once the lead is `qualified`, the default gate shows a clean Go.
  - `outreach.md` tells that agent to draft for Go leads, and to pull "all qualified" when a brief names none.
  - The only thing holding the lead back would be a table row that no script reads, in a file meant for actions waiting on a yes or no. A draft on hold isn't one of those actions yet.
- **It sets a precedent.** Each later lead with an awkward open check could get the same treatment, and `qualified` would stop saying anything.

### Can a draft be honest if the channel holds an official demo? No.

There are three ways to write the opener, and none works.

- **"I couldn't find a demo on your site or YouTube."** This is not honest today. We didn't look at the channel and fail to find one; we couldn't see it at all.
- **"Your site shows no video, as of 30 Oct."** This is literally true whatever the channel holds. If an official demo exists, though, it's the Groundrule case: gap rule 2 says a video in the wrong place is not a gap our product closes, and the question "is that on purpose?" becomes the embarrassing email round 1 stopped. Accuracy of the sentence doesn't rescue the reason for sending it.
- **Open on something else.** There is nothing strong to use. The Show HN and PeerPush signals are stale and can't be the reason for an email, Foresight has no date, and v0.59.0 is a routine release. The gap is the email.

So the draft is good only if the channel turns out clean. In that case it can be written afterwards at no cost, and if the channel holds a demo it gets thrown away. Drafting first buys nothing either way. It also breaks the record's own `next_action`, which puts the recheck and the Foresight date "before any draft".

### What waiting costs

Very little. The check takes a person about a minute. Repowise has no dated event that would pass while it waits, and the research stays fresh for 90 days. The cost is that this test run has no drafts, and that is a true result, not a failure. A polished draft would also make the human more likely to send, which is a good reason not to put one in front of them before the check.
