## Notes for the coordinator and the human (not fixes; this was the last revision)

**A. The URL opt-out check needs a fallback, or it pulls against hard rule 7.** Write-up "What a human should check" item 4 says "Do not record a LinkedIn or X opt-out by URL until T-20261103-11 is done". The schema (line 119) says "record a person's opt-out by email address where one exists". Neither says what to do when a person's only published contact is a LinkedIn or X profile. In the real pipeline, Cool Analytics (LinkedIn) and SpaceWise (`x.com`) are exactly that case. Hard rule 7 says to suppress an opt-out "at once". Followed literally, item 4 would leave such an opt-out unrecorded. On this branch, recording by URL is the safer act: it over-blocks every LinkedIn or X lead in the gate with a printed reason. But it doesn't stop `set-status` for that lead, and on main it blocks nothing. Suggested wording for the human check and the T-20261103-11 brief: "Until T-20261103-11 merges, record a LinkedIn or X opt-out by the person's published email if there is one. Otherwise record it by URL anyway, accepting the over-block, and also `hold` or disqualify that lead. Never leave an opt-out unrecorded." The brief's own wording for fix 2 is where this line came from, so the developer did what it was told.
