## Notes (non-blocking; for install or the human)

1. **Proposal line 37, claims table.** The table (claim, fact-sheet line, its words) is internal, like the box, but only the box has a removal rule. The approval row naming "the text to send" covers it in practice. To make it explicit, line 31 could say "Remove the box and the claims table before sending."
2. **Stalled line 22, Retire after an opt-out.** The host's `## When to stop` says "**Retire:** poor fit, so mark it `lost`". After `suppress`, an open lead is already `disqualified` (`leads.mjs` lines 265-269). The schema allows `disqualified` only to `new`, so `set-status ... lost` fails with "Nothing changed". That is safe, but the step can't be followed as written. Suggest: "`suppress` sets it to `disqualified`; don't also mark it `lost`."
3. **Stalled line 18, "left".** The dropped bullet said that a new person at the company is a new contact. Now a contact who left goes straight to Retire, which closes the company as a poor fit. That would be wrong if a successor exists. This traces to my r1 fix 15. The human or the coordinator decides whether to restore a short line such as "If someone else took over, they are a new lead."
4. **README line 23 (fix 7 row)** says "the smokescreen share says 'not enough data' until P4". The skill has no smokescreen-share line. The share is covered by line 22's "Never work out a rate yourself" and by README line 64. This is a wording echo only.
