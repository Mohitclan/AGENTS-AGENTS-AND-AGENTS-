## Method

- I built the pages with `collect()` and `renderPage()` and `renderLive()` from the branch, plus a forced `working` state. Then I rendered them with headless Chrome (`--headless=new`, `--virtual-time-budget`) from `file://` URLs. I started no server, so ports 4398 and 4399 were not touched. All files are in my scratchpad; nothing in the repo was edited.
- A 1440 px window gives a 1418 px inner width in headless Chrome. Window sizes were offset by 22 px to hit inner widths of 1280, 1279, 1330, 1218 and 1080 exactly.
- Fonts: `document.fonts.check('600 11px "IBM Plex Mono"')` was true in every run, so text measurements use the real face.

## What I could not verify

- **Real-time motion.** Frame production under virtual time is limited, so I could not watch a newly lit agent's halo actually pulse after a refresh. I could only see that it is a running, pending animation. A person should watch one live run.
- **Hover rendering.** The contrast figures are computed from the CSS and the computed colours. I did not force `:hover` in the browser.
- **Other browsers.** Chrome only. The in-place patch no longer detaches the SVG, so the r1 concern about Firefox and Safari SMIL on re-attach no longer applies, but this was not tested.
- **A real Jarvis run** and real phones, as in r1.
