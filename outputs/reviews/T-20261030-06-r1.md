## Judgement 4: the two questions (the human decides)

### Is a generic inbox like hello@groundrule.dev acceptable as a Go contact?

**View: yes, as an allowed contact, but it should cost reachability points and be chosen with care.**

- **Why it's allowed:** company policy bars personal addresses, not role addresses. It allows email. Hard rule 2 forbids guessing a named person's address, so a published general inbox is the honest route when no person's address is published. It meets the rubric's "published business channels with a source".
- **Why it's weak:** the schema gives reachability points for "a published route to the right person", and an inbox is not that. I suggest capping it at 5 of 10.
- **Which inbox:** prefer general or contact addresses over support@, which exists to help customers. So for Deplexo use contact@, not support@.
- **Wording:** a draft must not pretend to know the reader.
- **Rule of thumb to confirm:** generic general inbox: allowed. Support-only inbox: Conditional. No published route: Stop.

This question is moot for Groundrule, but the hello@ address did look well chosen: the /about page says "Built by the people who answer your email", and /pricing uses it for sales.

### Does a parent that "builds and runs software companies" still count as founder-led for segment 1?

**View: no, not on this evidence.** A parent or studio should not count unless a named founder is shown to run that product and the whole group is about 20 people or fewer.

- **Why segment 1 is defined this way:** "One decision-maker, same-day trial, Starter fits." That logic depends on a person launching their own product.
- **What the sources show:**
  - Vyana calls itself "a parent company for demanding software" and names four other businesses (Uplint, Stratl, PrimeStack, Rell), plus others "in build".
  - It states ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certification.
  - Its Stratl product also appears in the Metricall-AI-Lab GitHub org as `stratl-releases`, which suggests the two names are linked. The batch does not cover this.
  - That org's website, metricall.ai, describes "the parent brand building AI companies for enterprise and developers" and mentions "strategic capital, co-investment". It names neither Groundrule nor Vyana, so the link is not proven.
- **What cuts the other way:** the CIN (U62090DL2026PTC466597) has a 2026 year segment, which suggests the company is very young. So this could still be a few people. Nothing shows headcount or that it has outside funding, and the funded disqualifier also requires marketing and design staff, which is not shown.
- **The practical case:** a group that already made a polished 60-second video behaves like a team with its own launch resources.

If the human wants these reached anyway, I suggest a separate "studio or group" segment with its own message, not stretching segment 1.
