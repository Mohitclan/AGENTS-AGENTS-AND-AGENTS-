**2. Objection addition.**
- Markers: `pre_information` and `no_specifics` match rubric lines 51-52.
- `immediate_drop`: dropping the unvalidated 22-of-27 score is right. The replacement is a sound idea but not workable as written:
  - "by a person's reading" doesn't say who judges.
  - "asked one question" fails any answer that follows our own five steps, which ask at step 2 and check at step 4.
  - The marker can only be judged after 7 days of silence, so it can never lead to "skip the full answer": by then the full answer has been sent. Before answering, only two markers are known, so the flag needs both. After 7 days the "one calm question" overlaps the single "still a priority?" note in `stalled-deal` rule 5.
  - See fixes 7 and 8.
- Recovery rate: refusing to estimate is clear on line 26. The smokescreen share on line 41 ("from the script") has no such fallback (fix 7).
- **Minimum sample of 5: too thin for our volume.** We have 8 leads, none contacted, and a 20-email daily cap, so per-objection samples will sit in single figures for months, and at n=5 one reply moves the rate 20 points. The source itself is stricter in places: it drops the rate below n=10 when it has no engine (`SKILL.md` line 89), rates confidence "low" at 5-9, "medium" at 10-19 and "high" at 20 or more (`analyze.py` line 497), and gives no product verdict below 15 (`PRODUCT_MIN_N`, line 69). My suggestion for the P4 approver:
  - Below 10, print counts only ("3 of 7").
  - From 10, print a percentage with n.
  - Change no answer on the rate alone below 15.
  - This is the human's call through P4, so it is not a fix to this draft.

**3. Stalled-deal addition.** It is in the agent's own words (see the copying check), contains no pretext line, and requires an honest reason for writing (line 36).
