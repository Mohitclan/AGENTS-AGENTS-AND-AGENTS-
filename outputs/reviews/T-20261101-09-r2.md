### What is still imperfect

No revision follows, so these are for the person who reads the replies.

1. **Auto-reply or bounce (lines 18, 44, 62).** Neither is a human reply, so line 44 keeps the follow-ups going, while line 18's "not replied at all" and line 62 stop them. Read literally, line 62 sets `replied` and counts it, which disagrees with `reply-triage` lines 14-15 ("No reply"). With 7 or fewer contacted, one out-of-office could meet the threshold of 1. Treat auto-replies and bounces as not replies and leave them out of the metric. `replied` can't go back to `contacted` without `--force`, so catch this before setting the status.
2. **Answer plus interest (lines 60, 62).** "Not on GitHub, but tell me more" fits line 60 ("Nobody writes again") and line 62 ("a person decides"). Use line 62. The reader asked for something, and `reply-triage` rule 1 sends a doubtful case to a person.
3. **A bare "no" after a follow-up (line 60).** Emails 2 and 3 ask no yes-or-no question, so a "no" there can't be the answer line 60 describes, and it may be a refusal. Nobody writes again either way, but it isn't suppressed, so a later campaign could reach the same inbox. Whoever reads it should judge whether it is an opt-out (Hard rule 7).

A "yes" to Email 2 or 3 would get the terms again from line 59. That is harmless, since a person sends the reply.
