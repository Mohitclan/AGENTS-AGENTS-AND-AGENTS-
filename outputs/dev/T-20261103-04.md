# T-20261103-04: copy-check upgrade

## Problem

`scripts/lint-copy.mjs` fails on one banned word, a number with no source, and a short list of formulas. The surveys found gaps it never sees: superlative and comparison claims ("the only", "fastest"), quotations with no source, drafts full of mild filler wording, flat sentence rhythm, em dashes in bulk, links copied from an assistant's answer, and email sequences whose emails repeat each other. The human approved one package for all of them, with the rule that every new check is a warning and never a failure.

## Root cause

These checks did not exist. Evidence (read in this branch before the change):

- `evaluation/voice-rules.json` had `hard`, `flag` and `weak` lists only; no tier lists, no superlative list.
- `scripts/lint-copy.mjs` had seven `VOICE_PATTERNS`, `checkClaims` (number plus a metric word) and `checkEmails`, which reads each email alone (`splitEmails` then one pass per email).
- `scripts/check-links.mjs` line 25 strips trailing punctuation before testing a link, so it can never see punctuation stuck to a URL. That is why A18 and the URL check live in `lint-copy.mjs`.

Sources: `outputs/research/2026-11-03-gtm-survey-a-plugins-agents.md` (A1, A3, A18), `outputs/research/2026-11-03-gtm-survey-b-skills-connectors.md` (rows 6 and 16), `outputs/reviews/T-20261103-01-r2.md` (how they combine), `outputs/reviews/T-20261103-02-r2.md`.

| Check (code) | Rule | Threshold or setting | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 `superlative` | A claim word with no source marker within two lines and no "do not / never / avoid" on the line: "fastest", "number one" or "number 1", "better than", the "cheaper than" form, the hash-and-digit rank mark, "leading" in a claim shape ("a leading provider", "industry-leading"), "the only" followed within two words by tool, platform, product, app, solution, service, way, option, choice, company, provider, vendor or software | Same `sourceMarkers` list as `checkClaims` | `It is the fastest way to ship.` warns. `Aryan is the only person named` and `leading to a broken page` do not |
| A1 `unsourced-quote` | A quotation of 6 or more words, on a line with a speech cue (said, says, told, wrote, according to, testimonial, quote, described, or a dash and a name after it), no source marker within two lines | 6 words | `Asha said, "This tool saved our whole team hours every single week."` warns. A quoted page title with no speaker does not |
| A1 `vague-attribution` | "studies show", "experts say", "research shows" and similar with no source within two lines | none | `Studies show that launch videos help.` warns |
| A3 `vocab-tier1` | A word from `tier1` (25 entries with a plainer suggestion, for example delve, tapestry, pivotal, garner) | every use | `Let us delve into the repo.` warns with "try: dig into" |
| A3 `vocab-cluster` | Two or more different `tier2` words in one paragraph, list item or table row (24 words, for example robust, comprehensive, landscape) | `tierClusterMin` = 2 | `It is a robust and comprehensive tool.` warns; one word, or the two in different paragraphs, does not |
| A3 `vocab-density` | `tier3` words (20 common padding words, for example important, key, various) above a share of the text | 3% of words, at least 3 hits, at least 100 words | 20 repeats of "This is important and significant." warns |
| A3 `template-phrase` | 14 filler or leftover shapes: "plays a crucial role", "a testament to", "in the realm of", "ever-evolving", "when it comes to", "not only X but also Y", chatbot disclaimers and sign-offs, "Let's dive in", inflated importance, future-narrative closers, a generic conclusion opener, stacked hedges | one warning per match | `Launch videos play a crucial role in ever-evolving markets.` warns |
| A3 `rhythm` | Three sentences in a row starting with the same word (not "the", "a", "an", "it"); or 10 or more sentences whose lengths vary too little | spread (standard deviation over mean) under 0.40, mean at least 8 words | `We build videos. We write docs. We ship fast.` warns |
| B16 `template-phrase` | A list of three negations; two trailing "-ing" clauses piled onto one sentence. Merged into the same code. Types email, social and seo only (see noise) | none | `It needs no card, no setup, no catch.` in social warns; in general it does not |
| Em dashes `em-dash` | More em dashes (the long dash, U+2014) than `max(1, words / 1000 x emDashPer1000Words)`. A single dash is always allowed. An empty table cell holding a dash is not counted. Never a failure, never a ban | 1 per 1,000 words (`emDashPer1000Words` in `voice-rules.json`) | 3 dashes in 117 words warns; 3 dashes in 4,000 words does not |
| A18 `ai-url-param` | A URL query value that names an assistant: chatgpt, openai, perplexity, claude, gemini, copilot, grok, deepseek, poe, bard (with or without the domain). Every `--type`. Trailing punctuation is trimmed first, so `...?utm_source=chatgpt.com.` is still found | none | `https://example.com/p?utm_source=chatgpt.com` warns; `?utm_source=newsletter` does not |
| B6 `url-punctuation` | `--type email` only, inside each email: a URL ending in `. , ; : ! ? * _` | one warning per email | `Start the free trial at https://example.com/trial.` warns; the URL on its own line does not |
| B6 `email-sequence`, subjects | `--type email`: two emails with the same subject once "Re:" and "Fwd:" are removed and case is ignored | none | Email 2 `Re: your launch video` after email 1 `your launch video` warns |
| B6 `email-sequence`, step 1 | Email 1 asks for a call or meeting (wider than the existing `email-cta` booking pattern: "quick call", "15-minute chat", "hop on a call", "can we talk"). Skipped when the existing booking pattern already fired, so one email never gets two warnings for the same ask | none | `Can we hop on a quick call this week?` in email 1 warns |
| B6 `email-sequence`, repeated ask | Two emails in a row end on the same kind of ask. The ask is the last question (else the last sentence with a link), sorted into meeting, send, link or question | consecutive emails only | `Worth a look?` at the end of email 1 and again at the end of email 2 warns; with `Want me to send the sample?` between them it does not |

## Risks

- A warning is not a finding. Several patterns are blunt: "the only way" can be innocent, a quoted page excerpt with "said" nearby warns, the rhythm spread is calibrated on 28 internal files only. Anyone who treats a warning as a fail will block good copy. Exit 0 holds by construction, and 7 tests assert it.
- Warning counts go up for the evaluator and for agents reading lint output. Expect `url-punctuation` on every current outreach draft.
- `gate-log.jsonl` stores only the count of warnings, so the new ones raise that count for every logged run. `lint-copy.mjs mine` reads failures only and is unaffected.
- The word lists are ours and short on purpose. A legitimate engineering use of "robust" or "landscape" warns only when two such words share a paragraph.
- B16 shapes are skipped for `--type general`. A customer-facing page checked as general will not get them. Use the right `--type`.
- Em dash allowance has a floor of one. A 300-word text with one dash has a rate above 1 per 1,000 and still passes. That is my reading of "about one per 1,000 words"; the human may want it stricter.
- The email sequence checks assume headings like `## Email 2` (the existing `splitEmails` rule). A sequence without them is one email, so the cross-email checks do nothing for it.
- This branch sits on `49884cd` (P1, under review). If P1 is rewritten, rebase.
