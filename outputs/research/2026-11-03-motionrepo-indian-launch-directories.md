Peerlist ranks second: same weekly rhythm, but it needs a Verified individual profile and its audience skew is unsourced. Product Hunt ranks third: the strictest on how to ask for votes, and nothing on its page points to India. Constraints that apply to all three: no direct upvote requests, and a personal maker profile on Peerlist and Product Hunt. What could flip the order: an audience size for either of the first two, or evidence that Peerlist's verification is easy.
