# MotionRepo channel map: Indian founder-led product startups (T-20261030-02)

Date: 2026-10-30. First segment per `outputs/research/2026-10-30-motionrepo-market-brief.md`: launch-active, founder-led Indian software product startups. Every row was opened on 2026-10-30 unless noted (LaunchPad India could not be opened and is marked unverified). Nothing here has been posted or sent. All posting needs a human yes (CLAUDE.md hard rule 1). The call to action is "start the free trial" or "reply", never "book a call" (open-questions Q12). Audience figures are published by each venue and are not verified by me.

## Notes and cautions
- The best channel cannot be known without testing. Rank by effort: Peerlist and AIBoomi first (low cost, India), then one Product Hunt launch.
- Company policy: no competitors, no personal emails, and 20 cold emails and 20 LinkedIn messages a day. Community posting needs approval, and `ops/approvals.md` gets the request.
- LaunchPad India is unverified and left out of the priority list until a person has opened its site. r/developersIndia rules are also unverified.
- Any page that gave instructions to AI or visitors was ignored. None were found.
- The 403 and 429 errors (Peerlist guidelines, Indie Hackers, G2, GetApp, Canva, Reddit) mean those venues need a manual look by a person who may open them in a browser.
