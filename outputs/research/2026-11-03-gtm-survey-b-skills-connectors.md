**Free, open lead data as an MCP server.** Nothing I found is both free and a good fit.
- GitHub's official server is MIT (Copyright (c) 2025 GitHub) [R: raw LICENSE]. It has a `--read-only` flag on the local server, and it signs in by browser OAuth or a personal access token [R: its README, first 100,000 characters]. Public repo activity is already inferred evidence for segment 1 test 5 (memory decision 2026-11-01). The README I read doesn't say what it costs. Use OAuth, not a token, to keep to "no API key". The gain over fetching public GitHub pages is small [I].
- OpenRegistry says it offers 27 national company registries (UK Companies House among them) free, with live government calls [B: search result; licence and source not opened]. Registry data is company-level (officers, filings) and the registries it names are outside India [B].
- Bizfile MCP and CompanyScope need free third-party keys, or scrape, or have a paid tier [B]. OpenCorporates needs its own token [B].
- Verdict: no open lead-data MCP is worth adding now. Founder-led product makers (segment 1) are found on Product Hunt, Peerlist, Show HN and their own sites, which are not in company registries [I, medium].
