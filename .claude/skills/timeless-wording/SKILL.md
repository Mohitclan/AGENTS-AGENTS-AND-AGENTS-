# Timeless wording

The same slip has appeared in three agents' work: "card and UPI are coming", "doesn't fit yet". "Yet" and "coming" promise a roadmap we haven't confirmed.

1. **Describe what exists today, in the present tense.**
2. **Words to find and fix in customer text:**
   - coming, soon, yet, not yet, eventually, in the future, upcoming, on the way, planned;
   - currently, at present, now;
   - new, newer, latest, as of this writing, existing, old.
3. **For what isn't available**, use claim-check's forms:
   - "Not available today."
   - "MotionRepo doesn't do X. It does Y instead."

   Never imply a date.
4. **Unreleased or switched-off features never appear in public text**, not even indirectly ("we're adding...").
5. **When our own website uses future wording** (for example, the pricing page on UPI), customer text still states today's fact: "Payment is by invoice; card and UPI aren't available today." Quote the page, with its URL and date, only in internal notes.
6. **Allowed exceptions:**
   - dated items (release notes, a changelog, a dated post) may say "new in <version or date>";
   - a step may describe a change of state ("after a few seconds the status shows Ready").
7. **Self-review check:** search your draft for the words in step 2, then fix each hit or say why it's allowed.
