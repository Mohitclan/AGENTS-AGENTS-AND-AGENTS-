# Trial to paid

For a prospect who is talking to us and starting the free trial. The trial's length, credits and limits are in the fact sheet: read them there, never from memory. Sign-up nurture for strangers is cold-email's job, not this one.

1. **Who gets a plan.** Prospects in conversation with a dated need. If they have no date, or they're only comparing prices, let them trial on their own and skip the plan.
2. **Day 0 comes first: their own repo connected and a first output made.** Nothing else matters until then. If it hasn't happened by about day 3, one helpful note asking what's in the way.
3. **Success criteria in their words**, for example "a teaser for our launch on <date>". Work the dates back from their launch date, as the proposal skill's shared plan does. They agree to it; we never impose it.
4. **Two columns**: what they do, and what we do. Our column holds only what the fact sheet and docs allow (answers, docs links, a person's help on a call).
5. **The rhythm**, scaled to the trial's length:
   - Early: first output.
   - Middle: a second output, and their feedback.
   - Around the middle: a check against the success criteria.
   - Last days: the decision.

   Talk about the plan and price alongside, from the fact sheet only.
6. **Judge progress only from what they say**, or counts a person reads from product records. Never claim to see their usage. If they go quiet, one helpful note, then stalled-deal's single "still a priority?" note.
7. **Extensions and extra trial credits aren't on the fact sheet**, so they're `[NEEDS APPROVAL]`. If a person approves one, ask for something fair in return, such as a decision date or a feedback call. Never pressure.
8. **At the end**, a short feedback call. Mark each criterion met, partly met or missed. Then either recommend the plan that fits and write the proposal, or close honestly with stalled-deal's Retire or Watch.

## Never

- Invent urgency. The trial's real end date is fine to mention; "this offer ends Friday" is not.
- Use tracking, session replays or anything that watches what they do in the product.
- Add them to shared channels or lists without asking.

## How to tell it helped

Count, with `node scripts/stats.mjs` and its minimum sample:
- trials in conversation that reach Day 0 within three days, as X of N;
- trials that convert within the window, as X of N.
