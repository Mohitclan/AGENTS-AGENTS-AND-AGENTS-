# Meeting prep

## Tag every line

- `prospect_said`: their words, with where and when.
- `seller_said`: what we told them.
- `inferred`: our reading. Say why.
- `missing`: what we don't know yet.

Never turn an inference into something they said. Missing budget or authority is `missing`, not a bad sign.

## The page

1. **Who:** attendees, roles, and who can say yes.
2. **Known:** tagged facts from the lead's evidence and earlier messages.
3. **Goal:** the one decision this meeting should produce.
4. **Questions:** three to five, starting with what's missing. Ask what triggered this now, what they do today, and what happens if they do nothing.
5. **Objections:** the two most likely, with answers from the objection register.
6. **Agenda:** a 20-30 minute plan, agreed with them if possible.
7. **Next step to ask for:** specific, with a date.

## Before the meeting, score it

| Part | Max |
|---|---|
| Fit | 25 |
| Pain | 20 |
| Urgency | 20 |
| Authority | 15 |
| Next step | 10 |
| Evidence | 10 |

Below 50, ask whether the meeting is worth holding as planned, or should be a short discovery call instead.

## After the meeting

Draft a recap the same day: what they said, what we showed, what we promised, who does what by when. Promises beyond confirmed knowledge are `[NEEDS APPROVAL]`. Add new objections to the register with their exact words.
