# Hook bank

Don't take your first line. Write several, score them, keep the best.

## Seven families

Take one real buyer problem and try each:

1. **Pain callout:** names the problem as they'd say it.
2. **Identity:** "If you're a two-person team shipping every week..."
3. **Dream outcome:** the result they want, stated plainly.
4. **Proof-led:** a real number or event. No proof, no hook: cut it.
5. **Curiosity:** a fair question or surprising true fact, paid off in the next line.
6. **Common mistake:** what people get wrong, and that we can show.
7. **Speed or effort:** how little it takes, if true.

## Score each one, 0-2

| Test | 0 | 2 |
|---|---|---|
| Clarity | Must read twice | Instantly clear |
| Specificity | Could be anyone's | Only fits this buyer |
| Curiosity | Nothing to find out | Reader wants the next line |
| Proof | Empty claim | Backed by something true |

Keep hooks scoring 6 or more. Deliver three lengths: short (60 characters or less), medium (125 or less) and long (210 or less).

## Reject at birth

- Anything the rest of the piece can't deliver.
- Fake familiarity, false urgency or a misleading promise.
- Templates anyone could use, such as "Most people don't know this".
- Lines starting with "I", unless the story truly is yours.

## Before it goes out

Score the whole piece on the four U's, 1-4 each. A 4 is rare.

- **Useful** to this reader.
- **Urgent:** a real reason to act now.
- **Unique:** only we could say it.
- **Ultra-specific.**

Pass needs 12 of 16 for long content and 10 of 16 for email and social, with nothing below 2.
