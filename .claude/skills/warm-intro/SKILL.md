# Warm introductions

cold-email covers the first message to a stranger. This skill covers the better route: someone who knows both sides introduces us. Every message is a draft for a person to send.

1. **Who can introduce.** Only people a team member names, recorded as "told by <person> on <date>": the founders' own contacts, investors, community organisers, and early users once we have them. Never search for mutual connections; that needs a login.
2. **Run the target's checks first**: suppression, never-contact, and the lead gate. Nobody gets asked to introduce someone we shouldn't contact.
3. **Earn the ask.** Ask an introducer only after something useful has passed between you. Ask each one at most once a quarter, and never send the same ask to several introducers at once.
4. **Be specific.** Name one person (or one company and role), and why now, from the target's lead evidence with its signal class.
5. **Do the introducer's work for them.** Draft two parts:
   - **The ask** to the introducer: under 100 words, with an easy way to say no.
   - **A blurb they can forward as is**: three or four sentences. The product in one line from a confirmed fact-sheet line, why it fits this target, and one call to action the fact sheet allows (start the free trial, or reply). No results or "we helped X" until a proof-library row with permission exists.
6. **After the introduction**, draft a same-day reply:
   - thank the introducer and move them to Bcc;
   - one line tied to the target's situation and one call to action;
   - the cold-email opt-out line.

   If there's no answer, one follow-up after three working days with something new, then stop.
7. **If the introducer says no**: one thank-you line, and no new ask that quarter.
8. **Close the loop.** Draft a short update to the introducer at each stage: reply, call, trial, paid, or no.
9. **Log it** on the lead with `node scripts/leads.mjs`: the introducer, the date asked, and the outcome.

## Never

- Offer cash or rewards for introductions; any gift or perk is `[NEEDS APPROVAL]`.
- Like or comment on the target's posts first. That's a public action, and it needs a person's yes.
- Quote warm-versus-cold conversion figures; we have none of our own.
