# Proposal

A proposal is a draft. A person approves and sends it; sending is its own approval.

## Before you write

1. Read the lead's record and their messages. Their words are your material. Find their own decision date; if none, don't make one up.
2. Read the fact sheet and `knowledge/company.md`. Prices, limits and terms come only from there. List each claim with its fact-sheet line (claim-check).

## Shape

One to two pages, in this order:

1. **Today.** Their problem and its cost, in their words, with where and when.
2. **Wanted.** Their outcome and timing.
3. **Proposal.** One plan that answers each outcome. Live features only. If the product can't do what they asked, say so and list a handoff for the coordinator to pass to `developer` with their exact words.
4. **Price.** Quote the price and tax note as the fact sheet gives them. For a sum, show each line; never work out tax or a tax-inclusive total.
5. **Cost and value.** Only figures the buyer gave, each with its source. With none, ask for one at the call.
6. **If you wait.** The cost of delay in their terms. Never invent urgency.
7. **Why us.** What they reacted to, plus confirmed facts. Proof only from proof-library rows with permission; else the standard trial.
8. **Limits and terms.** A term not in confirmed knowledge is `[NEEDS APPROVAL]`: cancellation, refunds, SLA, security or data commitments, delivery dates. A billing period is not a cancellation term.
9. **Shared plan.** Work back from their date; each step has an owner and a date. With no buyer date, list steps and owners and ask them for a date at the call. Our timing promises need approval.

## Approval

Open with a box, "For the approver": each `[NEEDS APPROVAL]` item and reason, any discount asked for, open placeholders, `[UNCONFIRMED]` facts. Remove the approver box and the claims table before sending; the approval row in `ops/approvals.md` names the text to send.

- **Discounts:** none by default. Policy is in `knowledge/company.md`; any discount is `[NEEDS APPROVAL]`, never on monthly plans. With no annual plan, pass the request to the approvers.
- **Beyond standard** (longer trial, pilot, custom or self-hosted quote): `[NEEDS APPROVAL]`.
- Open markers mean it can't be sent. HubSpot is read-only: CRM changes are handoffs.

End with a claims table (claim, fact-sheet line, its words).
