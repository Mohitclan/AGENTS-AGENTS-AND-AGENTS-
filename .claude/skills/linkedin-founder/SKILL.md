# Founder-led LinkedIn

The founder's profile is the channel, not the company page. Write as a person who does the work.

## Setup

1. **Profile:** the headline says who you help and how. No buzzwords.
2. **Pillars:** three or four topics you can speak on from real work.
3. **Target list:** 15-25 accounts or people from the lead list.

## Writing a post

- The hook lands in the first 200 characters, before "see more". Use the hook-bank skill.
- One idea per post. Short lines. Real examples from real work.
- Put links in the first comment, not the body.
- End with a real question or a clear next step, never "thoughts?".
- Quotes and stories are drafts until the named person approves them.

## Rhythm

- Three to four posts a week beats daily filler.
- Ten to twenty real comments a day on target accounts' posts. Add something specific; never "great post".
- No engagement pods, bots or bought reach. No auto-DMs.
- Connection notes follow the outreach policy and the daily limits in `knowledge/company.md`.

## Avoid sounding generated

Cut formula lines such as "It's not X, it's Y", and lists of one-line lessons with no story. Use a specific detail only you would know. If a post could've been written by anyone, rewrite it.

## Measuring

Judge by conversations and profile visits from target accounts, not likes. Record what you posted and what happened, and name what you'll try next.

A person posts every draft. Nothing here is published by the team.
