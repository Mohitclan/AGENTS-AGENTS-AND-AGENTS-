# Competitive battlecard

One page per competitor. Everything dated, every claim sourced.

## Sections

1. **Who they are:** a line each on target buyer, positioning and price, with the date checked.
2. **They win when:** the situations where they're the better choice. Be honest; it builds trust.
3. **We win when:** the situations where we're better, each with proof.
4. **Trap questions:** fair questions a buyer can ask that reveal a real difference. Not tricks.
5. **What they'll say about us,** and the honest answer.
6. **Do not say:** claims we can't prove, or that are only hypotheses.

## Rules

1. Prices come from their own pricing page, dated. Recheck before any customer-facing use.
2. Never knock a competitor. Say what we do, not what they fail at.
3. A competitor gap labelled hypothesis can't be stated or implied, including through advice such as "need PDFs? use us."
4. No "cheaper than" claims unless both prices are in one currency at a dated rate.
5. If the buyer really is better off with them, say so and retire the lead.

## Win and loss notes

After every deal, record: who we lost or won against, the buyer's reason in their own words, and where that came from. Sort by reason, count with a denominator, and use the top reason to update this card.
