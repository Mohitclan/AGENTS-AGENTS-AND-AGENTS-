# outreach memory

What outreach has learned. Claude Code loads the first 200 lines into every outreach run.

- 2026-10-30 · A line labelled hypothesis in the fact sheet can't go in copy, even when it reads like positioning. Before saying how two outputs relate, check the product's own docs. (source: outputs/reviews/T-20261030-07-r1.md · by: coordinator)
- 2026-10-30 · Read what the lead sells before choosing a follow-up angle. Never pitch a problem their own product already solves. (source: outputs/reviews/T-20261030-07-r1.md · by: coordinator)
- 2026-10-30 · Each follow-up needs something of the lead's own, not only Email 1. An email that could go to any company scores as unpersonalised. (source: outputs/reviews/T-20261030-07-r2.md · by: coordinator)
- 2026-10-30 · lint-copy only counts words and looks for an opt-out in Email 1. Count every email yourself against the limit, and check the opt-out in each. (source: outputs/reviews/T-20261030-07-r1.md · by: coordinator)
- 2026-10-30 · Use one call to action per email. After moving a sentence, reread what the neighbouring lines now point at. (source: outputs/reviews/T-20261030-07-r2.md · by: coordinator)
- 2026-10-30 · Space follow-ups in working days, and check the reply window ends after the last send. (source: outputs/reviews/T-20261030-07-r1.md · by: coordinator)
- 2026-11-01 · When Email 1 asks a yes-or-no question, the opt-out word must differ from both answers. Walk every possible reply through the stop rules and the outcome metric, and write down what each one triggers. (source: outputs/reviews/T-20261101-09-r1.md · by: coordinator)
- 2026-11-01 · Greet by who the product's own pages say runs it: 'team' to a product made by one person shows nobody looked. A subject line is read before any 'if', so it can't assume what the body hedges. When two sequences share a claim, check each states the same limits. (source: outputs/reviews/T-20261101-09-r1.md · by: coordinator)
- 2026-11-01 · A reply table also needs a row for auto-replies and bounces (not replies, out of the metric) and a rule for which row wins when a reply fits two. Check what a bare no means after a follow-up that asked no yes-or-no question. (source: outputs/reviews/T-20261101-09-r2.md · by: coordinator)
- 2026-11-05 · New skills: use warm-intro whenever a person names someone who could introduce us (draft both the ask and a forwardable blurb), and event-outreach for any event a founder attends (targets from public speaker or demo lists only, never attendee lists). (source: asked by the human on 2026-11-05; skills from the web research in outputs/research/2026-11-05-agent-skills.md · by: claude-code)
- 2026-11-05 · Before drafting, read the reviews of any sequence you copy layout from, and never reuse a subject or line those reviews failed. A subject is read cold, so it can't sound like a notice about their code or data. (source: outputs/reviews/T-20261105-03-r1.md · by: coordinator)
- 2026-11-05 · Don't answer an objection the lead hasn't raised (certifications, SSO, what we lack): objection answers need a person's approval (fact sheet line 166). Spend a follow-up on a confirmed fact that helps them try, and link the page for the rest. (source: outputs/reviews/T-20261105-03-r1.md · by: coordinator)
- 2026-11-05 · Put each send condition on the row of the email it protects (a dated sighting's recheck on that email's row, placeholders on every row). Re-run lint and word counts after the last edit and count every email on one stated basis. (source: outputs/reviews/T-20261105-03-r1.md · by: coordinator)
- 2026-11-09 · In the If they reply table, a precedence sentence must match the row order: list rows in the order they win (opt-out, auto-reply or bounce, the specific answers, then anything else). The PuzariCloud layout's 'the higher row wins' was copied into two drafts where it sent a bare no to the wrong row. (source: outputs/reviews/T-20261109-01-L-ab940a31-r1.md finding 3; outputs/reviews/T-20261109-01-L-4ad62a74-r1.md finding 3 · by: coordinator)
- 2026-11-10 · When a stock sentence replaces a removed one, read the sentence before it: two of six Email 3 swaps repeated 'never writes to the repository' back to back, and 'It' after a URL reads as the URL. (source: outputs/reviews/T-20261110-16-L-64817c69-r1.md · by: coordinator)
