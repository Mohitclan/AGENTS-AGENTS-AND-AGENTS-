# Human-assisted lead research

Some sites don't load for agents, such as Peerlist and Product Hunt India. A person adds candidates here, and the lead-generator verifies them. A row is a candidate, not a fact.

**How to add one (about a minute each):**

1. Open [Peerlist Launchpad](https://peerlist.io/launchpad) or [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com), and look for recent launches by Indian software startups.
2. Check it fits segment 1: founder-led, about 2–20 people, a launch or release due in the next 60 days.
3. Add a row. Leave a cell blank if you don't know it. Don't guess.

A parent company or studio doesn't count, unless a named founder runs the product and the whole team is small.
